PREREQUISITE:

  MariaDB/MySQL with the rAthena schemas already loaded. Configure host,
  port, username, password, and database name under `databases.main` and
  `databases.log` in values.yaml. Credentials are stored in a Secret named
  "{{ include "rathena.fullname" . }}-secrets" and mounted into config files
  at deploy time.
